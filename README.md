# Global Leadership Competencies in International Higher Education

### Research Portfolio | Sammi Liu, PhD

**Educational Management | International Student Affairs | Global Leadership Development**

## 1. Project Overview

This repository documents selected frameworks, methodologies, and publicly shareable research outputs related to my doctoral research at Chulalongkorn University, Thailand.

**Dissertation Title:**

*Student Affairs Management Strategies for International Undergraduate Programs of Universities in Beijing, China Based on the Concept of Global Leadership Competencies*

The research explores how student affairs management can contribute to the development of global leadership competencies among international undergraduate students in Chinese higher education institutions.

## 2. Research Objectives

The study focused on:

1. Developing a conceptual framework integrating global leadership competencies with student affairs management.
2. Examining current and desirable conditions of student affairs management.
3. Identifying priority needs and relevant environmental factors.
4. Developing evidence-informed student affairs management strategies.

## 3. Global Leadership Competency Framework

The research examined six competency dimensions:

| Code | Dimension |
|---|---|
| GM | Global Mindset |
| ST | Self-Transcendence |
| GSR | Global Social Responsibility |
| CCC | Cross-Cultural Competence |
| GIC | Global Interpersonal Competence |
| GSI | Global Strategic Intelligence |

These dimensions provided the conceptual foundation for studying global leadership development among international undergraduate students.

## 4. Research Methodology

The doctoral research employed a multi-stage research design incorporating:

- Conceptual framework development
- Instrument design and expert validation
- Quantitative survey research
- Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA)
- Confirmatory Factor Analysis (CFA)
- Reliability and validity assessment
- Modified Priority Needs Index (PNI_modified)
- PEST environmental analysis
- SWOT and TOWS strategic analysis
- Expert consultation and focus group discussion

## 5. Research Context

The study focused on international undergraduate programs at universities in Beijing, China.

The research involved international undergraduate students and university personnel responsible for student affairs management.

## 6. Strategic Framework

The research developed the **GLOBE Compass** strategy framework to guide the integration of global leadership competency development into university student affairs management.

The framework considers different strategic responses and key phases of the international student experience.
